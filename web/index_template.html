Radial bubble momentum → acoustic pulse → piezoelectric recovery

DRIVEN LIQUID BUBBLE · NOT SPACETIME
A prescribed pressure actuator drives a physical gas cavity in water. Wall momentum pushes fluid outward; elastic compression and inertia drive collapse; outward acoustic energy is sampled at a collector 1 mm away, with a 1 mm² aperture. No electrical connection to the coil netlist is assumed.

Nuclear event: a Ni-63 nucleus of radius approximately 4.77 femtometers decays to Cu-63 and emits a beta electron plus antineutrino. The beta electron can be absorbed in silicon, create electron–hole pairs, and yield real DC electricity after diode and converter losses.

Not a femtometer-sized collector: conversion requires a physically larger semiconductor device. Neutrino energy, escaped particles, recombination and electrical losses are never counted as recovered output.

Independent finite reservoir: the Ni-63 source is not energized by the 35 V oscillator and is not a vacuum-energy source.
