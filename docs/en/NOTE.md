# Bubble Forge v0.20 — Active compression waves

Research software, not hardware verification. A finite-energy three-packet compressive wave train arrives during the forced bubble-compression phase. The coupled Keller–Miksis trajectory is calculated, but a shock is **not** resolved. Both the primary pulse and active-wave reservoir are deducted from the same 48 V converter. Maximum screened numerical contraction is about **49.58%**, with **−1.50407% operating-point net energy change** at the higher drive. This result is very close to the model validity boundary, assumes unmeasured coupling, and produces no net battery charge. The target of +0.01% energy gain remains unmet. See `README.md` and `research/wave_compression_reference_v020.json` for assumptions and reproducibility.
