Only the external gas vacuum can be swept to UHV/LEO levels. A sealed liquid cell retains its own independently set pressure. Exposed liquid at UHV is not treated as a stable water-bubble experiment. Calculated bubble sizes · no manual radius
The solver derives five candidate resonant radii from the coil-drive frequency, local absolute water pressure, and surface tension. Five is an internal numerical sampling grid, not a prediction of five physical bubbles. No bubble-count, initial-expansion, pressure-amplitude, acoustic-frequency, burst-cycles, or piezo-efficiency sliders are needed.

Geometry input from bubble equilibrium surface to receiver face. During expansion the clearance shrinks; acoustic capture uses the center-to-face distance. Closer may improve collection, but very small gaps are not validated.

The piezoelectric conversion (25%), spectral sampling bandwidth (0.7 octave), receiver area (1 mm²), and acoustic actuator pressure mapping are explicit uncalibrated model assumptions, not editable measurements. The pressure drive is capped at 5% of liquid pressure to stay in the conservative acoustic regime.

New target: net electrical energy ≥0.000% compared against every externally supplied electrical input and prepared bubble energy. This is a feasibility test, not a number forced by the optimizer. The best nonideal passive device is expected to remain negative. The interface reports the missing energy, mathematical upper bound, and modeled losses. The model does not demonstrate self-powering or a positive net gain. Electromechanical and acoustic transducer calibration, loading, reflections, real-time power metering, and 4 kV insulation qualification are still required.

Plasma mode now debits a hypothetical ionization-energy budget, evolves quasineutral ionized-pair density under expansion and recombination, and separates recombination and direct radiation losses. It still does not predict real ignition, exact species chemistry, or shocks. The size-grid results represent independently supplied reference events, not a measured population of bubbles. Each candidate is a gas bubble in water, with local-pressure-corrected linear resonance; it is not proof of long-term bubble stability. Acoustic scattering, gas diffusion, bubble nucleation, coherent group effects and acoustic interference are not solved. The input bus is not connected directly to the original coil: a hypothetical insulated converter regulates it to 35 V and accounts for losses. No high-voltage hardware has been validated. The 125 kHz oscillator uses a phasor approximation, not switching-resolved simulation. Bubble pressure and piezo response are assumed; the on-axis circular receiver uses finite-solid-angle acoustic capture with a 50% one-sided ceiling, and spacing below 10× maximum bubble radius is outside the receiver model. Shock formation is not resolved. No femtometer fluid-bubble recovery is credited.
